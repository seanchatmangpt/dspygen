Lifecycle

Standing

Contract

brce-intent

Construct bounded effect intents addressed exclusively to BRCE; DSPyGen performs no direct actuation.

evaluation-optimization

UNKNOWN

Evaluate DSPy programs against admitted datasets and bounded metrics before promotion.

ggen-projection

admitted

ALIVE

Project admitted RDF law into canonical Python and documentation surfaces without a generated directory.

lsp-verification

active

PARTIAL_ALIVE

Project admitted semantic constraints into diagnostics, navigation, and refactoring evidence.

mcp-boundary

active

PARTIAL_ALIVE

Separate read-only resources from broker-addressed tools and preserve typed failures.

pipeline-composition

admitted

PARTIAL_ALIVE

Compose dependency-closed DSPy programs with deterministic ordering and typed refusal.

receipt-replay

admitted

ALIVE

Bind source and projections with canonical BLAKE3 receipts that can be independently replayed.

signature-manufacturing

admitted

PARTIAL_ALIVE

Manufacture typed DSPy signatures and modules from admitted semantic declarations.

ALIVE requires a positive witness, negative falsifier, independent verifier, receipt verifier, and replay. Lifecycle and standing are orthogonal. All effectful intents are addressed to BRCE.
